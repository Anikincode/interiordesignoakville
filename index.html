About us

Every space tells a story. D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. creates interiors that join elegance, function and individuality, so that a home or business feels considered in every detail.

The firm is led by Lora Kiriboy, whose training in architecture and international project experience bring structural rigour to each layout, each material and each junction.

Residential, commercial, retail and hospitality interiors in Oakville and across the GTA. English, Russian and Hebrew spoken. More about the firm.
