Residential Interior Design in Oakville

A home in Oakville asks for more than good taste: it asks for a plan that holds together room by room.

In short

Residential interior design plans how a home looks and works: layout, materials, lighting, millwork and furnishings, drawn and coordinated as one scheme. D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. delivers this for Oakville kitchens, bathrooms, renovations, additions, custom homes and condos, from the first measurement to the final walk-through.

This page outlines what the work covers and links to each home service in detail.

What does residential interior design include?

A residential project begins with the space itself: how the household moves through it, where light falls, and what the structure allows. From that base the firm develops a layout, a material palette, a lighting plan and a furnishing direction, then records them in drawings that a contractor can build from.

The scope can be a single room or an entire home. What stays constant is the sequence: plan first, specify second, coordinate the build third.

How does the work differ between a renovation and a new build?

A renovation is shaped by what already exists, so the first task is measuring and documenting current conditions. A new build allows earlier influence over ceiling heights, openings and lighting infrastructure, before walls are framed. Both routes lead to the same drawing package and the same approval step.

The firm also coordinates additions, so new space matches the proportions and materials of the existing house.

Why does coordination matter as much as design?

Many residential problems are not failures of taste. They are failures of sequence: a light fixture ordered before the ceiling was framed, a cabinet drawn without the appliance specification. Single-point coordination, from drawings through site review, keeps the intent intact until the final day.

The work is led by Lora Kiriboy, founder of D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. Her training in architecture and international project experience inform every plan, drawing and site review.
