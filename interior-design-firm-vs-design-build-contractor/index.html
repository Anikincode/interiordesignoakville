Interior Design Firm or Design-Build Contractor?

Homeowners planning a renovation often have to choose between hiring a design firm and a design-build contractor.

In short

A design-build contractor handles design and construction under one contract, which is convenient and can be quick. An independent interior design firm designs and coordinates, while separate trades build, giving the owner independent advice and competitive bids. The better choice depends on how much control and independence the owner wants.

Below: how each route works, the trade-offs, and a way to decide.

How does a design-build contractor work?

One company designs and builds. The owner has a single point of contact and a single contract, and design and construction are naturally aligned. The trade-off is that the same company is advising on the design and pricing the build.

How does an independent design firm work?

The firm produces the drawings and specifications, solicits bids from several trades and coordinates construction on the owner's behalf. The firm's advice is independent of the builder's pricing. The owner holds more contracts and relies on the coordination.

How should an owner decide?

Choose design-build for a straightforward project where convenience matters most. Choose an independent firm where the design is demanding, custom millwork or specialized finishes are involved, or the owner wants competitive bids against a complete drawing package.
