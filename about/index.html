About D'Lora Luxe Design Inc.

Every space tells a story.

D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. is a luxury interior design firm serving Oakville and the Greater Toronto Area. The firm creates captivating, functional spaces that reflect each client's style while improving everyday life.

Who leads the firm?

Lora Kiriboy founded the firm in 2025. Her formal background is in architecture, and she has international project experience, including leadership roles on major projects. She works across residential, commercial, retail and hospitality interiors.

That architectural training shows in the drawings. Layouts, millwork and lighting are resolved on paper, to scale, before anything is ordered or built.

What does the firm do?

D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. plans and coordinates interiors from concept to construction. Residential work covers kitchens, bathrooms, renovations, custom homes, condos and additions. Commercial work covers restaurants, retail, offices and hospitality. Both rest on the same design services: space planning, custom millwork, lighting, furnishings, construction drawings, permits and coordination.

What is the firm's approach?

Four ideas guide every project. Luxury and function belong together, so a room has to work as well as it looks. Each space should tell the story of the people who use it. An international architectural perspective brings rigour to the details. And one team carries the work end to end, so nothing is lost between design and construction.

How does the firm work?

The firm plans the space, specifies the materials, draws the millwork and lighting, prepares the documents for approval and tender, and reviews the build on site. The full sequence is set out in the interior design process.

Where does the firm work?

The firm serves Oakville and the Greater Toronto Area as a service-area business. Virtual consultations are available for clients at the planning stage or outside the GTA.

Which languages are spoken?

English, Russian and Hebrew.

What has the firm completed?

Recent work includes a luxury boutique in Markham, offices in Oshawa and Markham, a hotel room design and a private residence in Vaughan. See them all on the projects page.
