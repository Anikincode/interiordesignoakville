Interior Design Services in Oakville

Each service below is a stage of one continuous process, and each can be engaged on its own.

In short

D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. offers the full sequence of interior design services in Oakville: space planning, custom millwork design, lighting layouts, furniture and décor selection, construction drawings, permit and tender coordination, and construction coordination. Clients can engage the whole sequence or a single stage.

Choose a service below for detail, or read the guides on cost, process and choosing the right kind of firm.

How do the services fit together?

Space planning comes first and sets the boundaries. Millwork, lighting and furnishings are designed inside those boundaries. Construction drawings record the decisions, permits and tender turn the drawings into a buildable project, and coordination protects the result on site.

Can a single service be engaged on its own?

Yes. A homeowner with a contractor already in place may need only drawings and lighting layouts; an owner with a finished shell may need only furnishings. The firm scopes the engagement to what the project requires.
