Custom Millwork Design in Oakville

Custom millwork defines the character of a room, and it is only as good as the drawings behind it.

In short

Custom millwork design produces the plans, elevations, sections, dimensions and material specifications that a fabricator needs to build cabinetry and built-ins. D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. draws Oakville millwork so the finished piece matches the design intent exactly and fits the room it was made for.

Below: what millwork drawings contain, which pieces benefit most, and where to read the planning guide.

What do millwork drawings contain?

Plans, elevations and sections with dimensions, tolerances, materials, hardware and finishes. They give the cabinet maker and installer one document to price and build from, which removes guesswork and limits change orders.

Which pieces benefit from custom millwork?

Kitchens, wall units and media walls, wardrobes and dressing rooms, entry storage, vanities, bars, banquettes and display fixtures. Anywhere a standard unit leaves gaps or wastes depth, a custom piece can resolve the problem.

How is millwork coordinated with lighting and services?

Integrated lighting, outlets, ventilation and appliances are located on the millwork drawings, so nothing is cut into finished cabinetry after delivery.

Which materials and finishes are specified?

Veneers, lacquers, solid timber, stone tops and metal accents are selected for the room and the way it will be used. Edge details, door styles and hardware are specified on the drawings, so the finished piece is consistent from one end of the run to the other.

The work is led by Lora Kiriboy, founder of D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. Her training in architecture and international project experience inform every plan, drawing and site review.
