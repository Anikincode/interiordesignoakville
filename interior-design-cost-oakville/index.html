Interior Design Cost in Oakville: What Affects the Price

The honest answer to what interior design costs is that it depends on scope, and scope can be defined precisely.

In short

Interior design fees in Oakville depend on the size of the project, how many rooms are included, whether construction drawings and coordination are needed, and the level of custom millwork. A complimentary 15-minute consultation establishes the scope before any fee is discussed.

Below: the factors that move a fee, what a quote should include, and how to compare two quotes fairly.

What factors affect the cost of interior design?

Project size and number of rooms; the depth of service, from a styling consultation to full drawings, permits and coordination; the amount of custom millwork; the number of revisions; and the timeline. A new build and a renovation also differ, because the survey and coordination work differ.

What should a quote include?

A written scope listing deliverables (plans, elevations, millwork drawings, lighting layout, specifications), what is excluded, how many revision rounds are included, and when payments fall due. Ambiguity in a quote usually becomes a cost later.

How should two quotes be compared?

Compare scope before price. A lower fee that leaves out construction drawings or site review may cost more once the project is underway. Ask each firm to price the same list of deliverables.

Is there a lower-cost way to start?
