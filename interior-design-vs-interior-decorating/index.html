Interior Design vs Interior Decorating: What Is the Difference?

The two terms are often used as if they mean the same thing, and they do not.

In short

Interior design plans the structure and function of a space: layout, millwork, lighting, materials and construction drawings. Interior decorating selects and arranges the surface layer: furniture, colour, accessories and styling. Many homes need both, in that order, because decoration works best inside a plan that already functions.

Below: what each covers, how to decide, and how the two fit together on one project.

What does interior design cover?

Space planning, custom millwork, lighting layouts, material specification, drawings for permits and construction, and coordination with trades. It changes how a space works as well as how it looks.

What does interior decorating cover?

Furniture selection, colour, textiles, art and accessories: the layer that makes a finished room feel personal. It does not normally alter walls, services or built-in elements.

Which does your home need?

If walls move, cabinets are built or lighting is rewired, the work is design. If the room is structurally complete and only needs to feel finished, the work is decorating or styling. Where the room needs both, the plan comes first, and the firm offers a focused styling consultation for the second stage.
