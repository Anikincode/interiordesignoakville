Permit and Tender Coordination in Oakville

An approved design is not yet an approved project.

In short

Permit and tender coordination prepares the documentation that building departments require, tracks approvals, and then collects and compares bids from qualified trades. D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. manages both for Oakville projects, so approvals are in place before construction and contractors are chosen on capability as well as price.

Below: what the permit stage covers, how bids are compared, and what remains to be confirmed.

What does permit coordination cover?

Preparing and submitting documentation to the municipal building department, coordinating with zoning or planning offices where needed, and adjusting drawings to satisfy code. Conditions attached to a permit are addressed at once rather than left for the contractor to discover mid-build.

How does tender coordination work?

Bids are requested from qualified trades against one drawing package, then compared on scope, capability, availability and fit as well as cost. The comparison is presented clearly so the client can decide with the whole picture.

Why do approvals come before construction?

Permit delays are among the most common and most avoidable causes of a stalled project. Securing approvals first keeps every later stage on schedule.

The work is led by Lora Kiriboy, founder of D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. Her training in architecture and international project experience inform every plan, drawing and site review.
