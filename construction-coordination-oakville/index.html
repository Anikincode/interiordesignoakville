Construction Coordination in Oakville

The design is only as good as the building that follows it.

In short

Construction coordination manages communication between trades, reviews site conditions and checks progress against the approved drawings, so the design intent survives construction. D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. provides it for Oakville projects from first fix to final installation, ending with a room by room walk-through.

Below: what coordination involves, how issues are resolved on site, and how a project is closed out.

What does coordination involve?

Scheduling and sequencing trades so they do not conflict, answering questions as they arise, and keeping a direct line between client, contractor and the design team. A clear drawing package is what makes this efficient.

How are site issues handled?

Site visits and progress reviews catch differences between drawings and execution early, when they are cheap to correct. Decisions are recorded so that the same question is not asked twice.

How is a project closed out?

A final walk-through is made in person, room by room, measuring millwork alignment, finish quality and lighting function against the approved drawings. Remaining discrepancies are corrected, and a completion report records what was built and the relevant warranty and maintenance information.

The work is led by Lora Kiriboy, founder of D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. Her training in architecture and international project experience inform every plan, drawing and site review.
