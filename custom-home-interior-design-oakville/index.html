Custom Home Interior Design in Oakville

The best moment to design an interior is before the house exists.

In short

Custom home interior design means engaging while the house is still on paper, when ceiling heights, openings, millwork scope, lighting infrastructure and material selections can still shape the structure. D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. works alongside the builder so an Oakville custom home is resolved inside as carefully as outside.

Below: what early involvement changes, how the firm works with a builder, and what a finished specification covers.

Why involve an interior design firm before construction?

Interior decisions have structural consequences. A tall bookcase wall needs backing in the framing, a feature light needs a ceiling that allows it, and a large format tile needs a flat floor. Raised at the plan stage, these cost nothing. Raised during finishing, they become change orders.

How does the firm work with the builder and architect?

The firm coordinates through the drawing package: interior elevations, millwork details and lighting layouts are issued so that the builder, electrician and cabinet maker all price and build the same thing. Lora Kiriboy's architectural training makes the conversation with other design professionals direct.

What does the finished specification include?

A room by room schedule of finishes, fixtures, millwork and furnishings, with the drawings that locate each one. It becomes the reference for tender, installation and the final walk-through.

The work is led by Lora Kiriboy, founder of D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. Her training in architecture and international project experience inform every plan, drawing and site review.
