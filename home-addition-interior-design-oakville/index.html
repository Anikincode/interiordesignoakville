Home Addition Interior Design in Oakville

An addition should look as though the house always had it.

In short

Home addition interior design coordinates the inside of new space with the existing home, matching proportions, materials, lighting and circulation. D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. plans Oakville additions so the joins between old and new are resolved in the drawings, and the result feels intentional rather than added on.

Below: where additions go wrong, how the firm matches old and new, and when to involve the interior plan.

Where do additions usually go wrong?

At the transition. Floor levels that do not meet, ceiling heights that change without reason, and finishes that almost match all signal that new space was bolted on. These are planning errors and they are visible from the front door.

How are old and new spaces matched?

The firm records the proportions, trim profiles, floor materials and lighting character of the existing rooms, then carries the right elements into the addition and updates the rest deliberately, with the join designed rather than discovered.

When should the interior plan begin?

At the same time as the architectural drawings. Window positions, ceiling heights and structural openings set what the interior can do, and they are inexpensive to adjust on paper.

The work is led by Lora Kiriboy, founder of D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. Her training in architecture and international project experience inform every plan, drawing and site review.
