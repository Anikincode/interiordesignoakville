Furniture and Décor Selection in Oakville

Furniture selection is an exercise in scale, proportion and function before it is anything else.

In short

Furniture and décor selection specifies, sources and manages the procurement of furnishings, coordinating lead times, delivery and installation. D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. selects pieces for Oakville interiors against the approved plan, so each item fits its space, suits its use and arrives in the right order.

Below: how pieces are specified, what procurement covers, and how styling is added at the end.

How are furnishings specified?

Each piece is checked against the plan for dimensions and clearances, then against the room's materials and light for finish and tone. The result is a schedule that records what is needed, in what size and material, and where it goes.

What does procurement involve?

Sourcing, ordering and tracking lead times, which can be long for custom and imported pieces, then coordinating delivery and installation so the project does not stall waiting for a single item.

How does styling finish a room?

Accessories, art, textiles and the last adjustments in arrangement are decided at the final stage. A separate styling consultation is available for rooms that are structurally complete but not yet fully realized.

The work is led by Lora Kiriboy, founder of D'Lora Luxe Design Inc. Her training in architecture and international project experience inform every plan, drawing and site review.
