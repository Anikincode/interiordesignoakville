What happens in the 15-minute consultation?

The call establishes what the project involves, how large it is and when it needs to be finished, and whether the firm is the right fit. There is no charge for it.

What should you prepare?

A floor plan if one exists, photographs of the space as it stands today, a rough budget range and a target completion date. Notes on what works and what does not are just as useful.

Where does the firm work?

Oakville and the Greater Toronto Area. Clients outside the GTA, or at an early planning stage, can book a virtual consultation.

When is the firm available?

Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. The firm is closed on Saturday and Sunday. Online service hours are also 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Which languages are spoken?

English, Russian and Hebrew.
